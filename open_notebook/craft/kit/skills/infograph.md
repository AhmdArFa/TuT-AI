# Infograph skill

Call the `generate_document` tool with feature `infograph`.

- Content first: a title, two columns of 3 or 4 numbered blocks (bold heading,
  2 or 3 short lines each) and a one-line verdict.
- Layout: speech-bubble title, central dashed divider, rounded column headers,
  numbered badges, one doodle per block placed near its text, curved arrows,
  verdict box bottom centre.
- Noir sketchnote: Midnight ink and Pulse accent on white (light, default for
  print) or White and Pulse on Midnight (dark).
- Audit: export the PNG and check overlaps, clipped doodles, text outside its
  block and unbalanced columns.
